All Sensors is a focused utility for seeing — really seeing — what every sensor on your iPhone or iPad reports, in real time.

Twenty-one sensors, one clean dashboard:

• Motion: accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, device motion (fused IMU), pedometer, altimeter, motion activity classifier
• Location: GPS coordinates, speed, altitude, heading (true & magnetic), barometric pressure
• System: battery level & charging state, thermal state, CPU cores & load, memory pressure, storage usage, screen brightness
• Connectivity: Wi-Fi network type, cellular carrier & radio technology, Bluetooth state and discovered peripherals
• Hardware: camera capabilities, lens enumeration, microphone audio levels, ambient light estimate, proximity, torch level

Each sensor has its own detail screen with three-axis charts, gauges, raw value readouts, and a live magnitude calculation. Tap into any sensor to see what's actually being read by the underlying iOS APIs (CMMotionManager, CLLocationManager, CMAltimeter, CMPedometer, CMMotionActivityManager, CBCentralManager, NWPathMonitor, AVCaptureDevice, ProcessInfo, UIDevice, HKHealthStore).

▼ Show-Off Mode
Tap the Show-Off banner on any sensor detail screen and the device transforms. The sensor takes over the full screen with a theatrical visualization — flight HUDs, compass roses, gravity wells, oscilloscopes, gauges, lighthouses. There are 74 unique full-bleed visualizations across 21 sensors. Swipe horizontally to switch sensors, swipe vertically to cycle variants, tap the grid icon to jump to any of them. It's a great way to demo what your phone is doing right now to a friend, a class, or yourself.

▼ Logger
A real recorder. Pick any subset of sensors, set a sample rate (60 Hz down to 1 Hz, per sensor), tap record, walk around. When you stop, your session shows up in a browser with timeline, sensor-by-sensor breakdown, and one-tap export to CSV or JSON for analysis in Numbers, Excel, Python, R, or whatever you use. Logs are stored in a local SQLite database under your app's Documents folder; nothing is uploaded.

▼ HealthKit
The Health tab shows your steps, heart rate, active energy, distance, and sleep — read directly from HealthKit with a permission scope that's read-only by default. Toggle write access on if you want the app to record workouts.

▼ Siri & Shortcuts
"Get Sensor Reading", "Start Sensor Recording", "Stop Sensor Recording", and "Export Sensor Data" are wired as App Intents. Add them to a Shortcut, ask Siri, automate from Focus modes.

▼ Privacy
Every reading stays on your device. There is no analytics SDK, no ad SDK, no crash reporter, no remote backend. The app makes no outbound network requests except a single optional ping to 1.1.1.1 when you ask the Network sensor to test connectivity.

Built for developers, makers, students, hardware hobbyists, geocachers, weather watchers, and anyone who's ever wondered what their phone really knows about itself.
