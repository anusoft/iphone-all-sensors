## Pricing

| Field         | Recommended (you can change anytime) | Why |
|---------------|---------------------------------------|-----|
| **Price tier**| **Free** (Tier 0)                     | Lowest barrier for first reviews + word-of-mouth. Switch to a paid tier later by submitting a price change (no re-review). |
| **In-App Purchases** | None                            | The app has no IAP wired and no upgrade path. |
| **Subscription** | None                                |  |

If you want to charge:
- $1.99 (Tier 1) is the conventional "lightweight utility" price.
- $2.99 (Tier 2) signals more polish without scaring anyone.
- $4.99 (Tier 3) starts to feel "professional" — fits if you grow the logger features.

## Availability — countries / regions

Default: **All countries and regions**.

Reasons to scope down:

- **Localization gaps** — if your description is English-only, App Store auto-translates the metadata for some stores. That's acceptable for this app.
- **Legal blockers** — none apply to this app.

If you want to start narrow and expand later, "United States" only is the safest first launch. You can flip to "All" any time without re-review.

## Pre-orders

**Off**. Pre-orders make sense for content apps with marketing campaigns. For a v1.0 utility, just publish.

## Availability date

| Option | When to use                                  |
|--------|----------------------------------------------|
| **Manually release this version** | Default. After Apple approves, you push a button to publish. Lets you coordinate with social posts, press, etc. |
| **Automatic release** | Approved → live immediately.                 |
| **Schedule release** | Approved → live on a date you pick.          |

For v1.0, **Manual** is the safest — gives you a few hours after approval to make sure the build looks right on the store page before going live.

## Tax & Banking (App Store Connect → Agreements, Tax, and Banking)

Required if your price is anything other than Free. For Free apps you only need the **Free Apps Agreement** signed.

- [ ] Paid Apps Agreement (only if you charge)
- [ ] Tax forms — `[FILL: W-9 if US, W-8BEN-E if non-US individual, W-8BEN if non-US org]`
- [ ] Bank account — `[FILL: routing/account info; iDEAL or local equivalent]`

These are NOT required to *submit* a free app for review, but you cannot collect any money until they're filled. Recommended to set up the Free Apps Agreement before you submit.
