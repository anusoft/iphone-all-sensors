# Privacy Policy for All Sensors

**Last Updated:** May 5, 2026

## Introduction

All Sensors ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS app.

## Data We Collect

All Sensors reads data from your iPhone's built-in sensors, including:

- **Motion Sensors:** Accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, device motion, pedometer
- **Location:** GPS coordinates, altitude, speed, compass heading
- **Environment:** Barometric pressure, proximity sensor, ambient light
- **System:** Battery level, processor usage, memory, storage, thermal state
- **Connectivity:** Bluetooth status, WiFi network information
- **Camera:** Camera availability, torch/flashlight status
- **Health:** Steps, heart rate, activity data (only with your permission via HealthKit)

## How We Use Data

**All sensor data is processed locally on your device only.**

We use this data to:
- Display real-time sensor readings in the app
- Record sensor data for your personal use (when you explicitly start a recording)
- Generate diagnostic reports (when you run hardware tests)
- Export data in CSV or JSON format (when you choose to export)

## Data Storage

- **No server transmission:** We do not send your sensor data to any remote servers
- **Local only:** All data stays on your device
- **No account required:** You can use the app without creating an account
- **Your control:** You can delete any recorded data at any time

## Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties. The only way data leaves your device is when you explicitly choose to:
- Export data to your Files app
- Share data via the iOS share sheet (AirDrop, Messages, Mail, etc.)

## Permissions

The app requests access to sensors and data only when needed:
- Location: To show GPS and compass data
- Motion & Fitness: To show accelerometer, gyroscope, and activity data
- Camera: To show camera capabilities and control the torch
- Microphone: To display audio input information
- Bluetooth: To show Bluetooth status and scan for devices
- Health: Only if you choose to connect HealthKit data

You can deny any permission and still use most features of the app.

## Third-Party Services

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not contain any third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is intended for general audiences.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

- Email: support@1moby.com
- Website: https://1moby.com

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR, CCPA, or other privacy laws:
- Right to access your data
- Right to delete your data
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control. Simply delete the app to remove all stored data.
